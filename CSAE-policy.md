
# Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) Compliance Declaration
**Application Name:** Awful.apk
**Application Type:** Mobile browser client for accessing the Something Awful forums

---


## 1. Zero-Tolerance Policy
Awful.apk maintains a strict zero-tolerance policy toward Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE). The application does not create, host, distribute, or promote any content involving the sexual exploitation or abuse of minors.

---

## 2. Nature of the Application
Awful.apk is a browser-type application that provides a mobile interface for accessing the Something Awful forums. 
- The app does **not host or store content** on its own servers as it has none.
- The app acts solely as a **client interface** that renders content from external websites.
- The app does **not collect, retain, or process user-generated content** independently of the accessed website outside of message drafts which are stored locally on the device.

---

## 3. Content Responsibility and Limitations
- All content accessible through Awful.apk originates from third-party sources (i.e., the Something Awful forums at https://forums.somethingawful.com ). 
- Content moderation, storage, and enforcement of community guidelines are the responsibility of the forum operators.
- Awful.apk does not have direct control over third-party content but does not provide any features designed to facilitate illegal or abusive activity.

---

## 4. Safety Measures
Although Awful.apk does not host content, the following measures are in place:
- The app does not include features that enable anonymous content hosting, file sharing, or direct user-to-user media transfer.
- The app respects platform-level safety protections (e.g., OS-level reporting and blocking tools).
- The app does not include functionality intended to bypass moderation systems of the accessed website.

---

## 5. Reporting and Cooperation
- Users are encouraged to report harmful or illegal content directly to the Something Awful forum moderators or administrators via the report function.
- Where applicable, users may also report illegal content to relevant law enforcement authorities.
- The developer will cooperate with lawful requests from authorities regarding misuse of the application.

---

## 6. Compliance with Google Play CSAE Policy
Awful.apk complies with Google Play’s Child Safety Standards by:
- Prohibiting any use of the app for CSAE-related purposes
- Not enabling the creation, storage, or dissemination of CSAE content
- Providing transparency about the app’s limited role as a content viewer
- Maintaining a commitment to user safety and legal compliance

---

## 7. Contact Information
For CSAE-related concerns regarding the application please contact the developer contact via the Google Play Store App page information

---

## 8. Ongoing Commitment
The developers of Awful.apk is committed to maintaining compliance with applicable child safety laws and Google Play policies. This declaration will be updated as necessary to reflect changes in regulations or app functionality.
